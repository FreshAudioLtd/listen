🎙️ Audio Stream

Stream audio from one device to up to 5 listeners, over cellular or wifi.

📡 Broadcast Send audio from this device's microphone or audio input. 🎧 Listen Join a broadcast using a room code or link.

Both devices need an internet connection (wifi or cellular data) — they don't need to be on the same network. Keep this page open and your screen on while broadcasting or listening for the most reliable connection.
