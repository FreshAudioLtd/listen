Fresh Audio ISO Mixer

Stream isolated mic feeds from one device to up to 5 listeners, who each choose which channels to hear.

📡 Broadcast Send individual mic channels from a multi-channel input (e.g. Dante Virtual Soundcard). 🎧 Listen Join a broadcast and tap to listen to any combination of channels.

Both devices need an internet connection (wifi or cellular data) — they don't need to be on the same network. Keep this page open and your screen on while broadcasting or listening for the most reliable connection.
